# Homepage: acceptance criteria

1. The header shows the logo, the main navigation (Features, Resources, Migration, Pricing), "Log in" and a "Start free" button.
   Below 860px the navigation is hidden; below 480px "Log in" is hidden too.
2. The hero headline reads "Email marketing without the hassle." with "hassle." in orange.
3. Submitting the hero email form with a valid address shows "Thanks! This is a design preview, so no email was sent."
   An invalid address shows no confirmation. Nothing is sent anywhere.
4. Sections appear in this order: hero, ratings, features, automation, pricing, migration, integrations and MCP, support, final call to action, footer.
5. Pricing shows the free plan at $0 and the Standard ($7) and Professional ($15) paid plans, with Professional marked "Popular".
6. Every image loads, and every logo has alt text unless it sits next to its name.
7. At 390px wide there is no horizontal scroll and no text spills out of its button, tile or card.
8. All text meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1, or 3:1 for text 24px and larger).
